Trailmix

1. Drag Trailmix to the Applications folder.
2. Open it. macOS will say it "can't be verified", because this build isn't signed by an Apple developer yet.
   To allow it once:
     System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> scroll down -> "Trailmix was blocked" -> Open Anyway.
   (Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Trailmix.app )
3. Allow Microphone access when asked. To record the other side of calls (Zoom, Teams, FaceTime),
   also allow "System Audio Recording" when macOS asks.

Trailmix lives in your menu bar. Everything stays on your Mac.
